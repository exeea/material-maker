# Cookbook

55 finished materials, one `.ptex` graph per material, grouped by category. Each graph has a recipe card
(`<name>.md`) that says what it is, which nodes and settings make it work, and where to look in the graph.

Open a graph in Material Maker to study or reuse its node network. Over MCP, `list_examples` lists them with
their category and description, and `load_graph` opens one.

The graphs and recipe cards come from Grayson Chalmers'
[Tool-MaterialMaker-MCP](https://github.com/graysonchalmers/Tool-MaterialMaker-MCP), MIT licensed (see
`LICENSE`).
